Travel advisors

House of Mosaic is a six-room art villa and gallery in the quiet streets of Oranjestad, minutes from the beaches and the airport. The house has welcomed guests since 2004, and its team has designed weddings and celebrations since 1994.

Why advisors place clients here

Central, and quiet: the house sits in the residential streets of Oranjestad, a few minutes from the beaches and from the airport.

Small enough to arrange by hand: six rooms, so every arrival is set up individually rather than processed.

All love is celebrated here: House of Mosaic proudly hosts LGBTQ+ weddings and events.

An art villa, not a hotel: six one-of-a-kind rooms, an art gallery and a sculpture garden, hand-decorated by the owner.

Flexible where large properties are not: a small house can shape the details around your client.

A direct line to the owner: you deal with Fernando Mansur, who owns the house and is always somewhere in it.

The house at a glance

  • Rooms Six one-of-a-kind rooms, each with its own character, in a 1950 family house.
  • On the property An art gallery, a sculpture garden, and a garden venue for events.
  • Events Weddings, vow renewals, private celebrations and themed dinners, styled in house.
  • Experiences Spa in a Bed rituals and the Bar Bizarro cocktail collection, both by arrangement.
  • Residency Write on Aruba, in partnership with the Hudson Valley Writers Residency.
  • Guests Adults, 21 and over. Check-in 4 to 7 pm, check-out 11 am.
  • Address Schotlandstraat 4, Oranjestad, Aruba.
